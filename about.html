About

Hi, I'm Daniel. I work in product and build out of curiosity.

Product lead, 9+ years across B2B and B2C. Also a reader who keeps a second brain.

Product work and curiosity

In Nov '24 I built my first app, SmartChef, by prompting an AI coding tool page by page. I had no coding background. The app reached the App Store.

My product work runs from understanding a user problem to deciding what to do and checking the result. AI helps me research, challenge assumptions and make that work easier to pick up again.

Side projects gave me room to experiment. Sparktale is live and built with a partner developer. Wellness Coach is a work in progress. I stopped Parapet with a written reason. I share what I learned alongside the tools.
