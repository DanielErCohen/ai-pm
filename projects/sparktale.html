What I tried

Custom children's books take weeks to make, so Sparktale turns a child's name, a photo and a two-minute form into an illustrated storybook with the child as the main character.

  • Books come as a printed hardcover or softcover through print-on-demand, plus a PDF, in five languages: English, German, Hebrew, Spanish and Portuguese.
  • A 5-step wizard collects the child, the book type, the theme, the art style and a custom touch.
  • Sign-up comes after the reader has seen the book.
  • The character stays consistent across pages and single illustrations can be regenerated.
  • A page-flip reader supports right-to-left for Hebrew.
  • Prose lives in a knowledge-base repo, code-coupled docs in the code repo and binaries in Drive. Decisions and reviews are dated documents.

Questions I asked along the way

  • 20 Aug '26

    Should the book jacket preview be on by default?

    Option rejected
    Default off, which would ship the old preview of painted sample pages and make the jacket opt-in. Any environment that forgot the setting would then run the old path without anyone noticing.
    What happened
    The brief recommended on by default, with a server-side switch that brings back three painted sample pages without a code change. The switch lets me restore the old preview; the brief records no conversion result.

What happened

Still running. Sparktale is live at sparktale.ai and has paying customers.

  • Reaching parents was the hard part.
