How I work

How I use AI across product work

You frame the problem, make the call and sign off. At each step AI adds sources or a counter-argument before anyone else sees your draft. Start with the part of product work you are doing now.

  • Steps 1–3

    Understand the problem

    • 1Frame the problemAI more sources, and a counter to your framing
    • 2Learn from usersAI personas from your segment rehearse the flow
    • 3Define successAI checks your metric against the question and the data

    Leave with a problem statement and a way to judge progress.

  • Steps 4–5

    Choose and shape the work

    • 4Make a decisionAI the strongest counter, then a stakeholder panel
    • 5Scope the workAI simulated engineering and design readers question your brief

    Leave with a reason for the work and clear acceptance criteria.

  • Steps 6–9

    Deliver and learn

    • 6Support deliveryAI checks each change against the brief
    • 7Check the workAI a red team that did not build it
    • 8Prepare the releaseAI simulated users read the release note
    • 9Measure and learnAI checks the inputs and argues with your reading of the result

    Leave with review evidence and a next decision.
