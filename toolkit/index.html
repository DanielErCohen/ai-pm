  • Your own week of PM work: Personal development review.
  • Pages and podcasts worth keeping: Page-saving extension.

Using these at work

Whatever you paste goes to your AI provider, so decide what to paste first.

  • Never paste health records (in the US, protected health information under HIPAA), financial account data, personal identifiers, credentials or anything your employer classifies as confidential.
  • Removing names does not make a text anonymous: a role, a date or an amount can still point to a person or a customer.
  • Check your company's AI tool settings first: an enterprise plan, data retention and whether training on your chats is off. Follow its policy.
  • If you pasted something you should not have, delete the chat and tell whoever owns data protection at your company.
