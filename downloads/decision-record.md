# Decision record

Decision owner:
Date:
Status: open | decided | changed after review
Review on:

## Call
What will we do, and what will we leave out?

## Evidence
What did we observe? Include the source and date.
Separate observation, stated opinion, desk research and assumptions.

## Strongest counter
What is the best argument against this call?
What did the person challenging it see that we missed?

## Test and decision rule
Who or what will we test, against which baseline, and on what date?
Write the result that means continue, change, or stop before the test.
Why is this threshold enough for the decision at stake?

## Wrong if
What other observation would make this decision wrong?

## Review result
What happened, what remains unknown, and what is the decision now?
Keep the original rule visible if you change it.
